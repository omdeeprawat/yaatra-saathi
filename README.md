An AI-powered yaatra companion app for the Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra.

Nanda Devi is also known as Mata Parvati and the Nanda Devi Raj Jat Yatra is a pilgrimage that takes place every 12 years in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. The yatra involves a trek to the Nanda Devi peak, which is considered sacred by the local people. The pilgrimage is a significant cultural and religious event for the people of Uttarakhand and attracts thousands of devotees from all over India and abroad.
